Yatekomo Privacy Policy

At "Yatekomo" we place great importance on current regulations regarding privacy and data protection. In this sense, protecting the security, integrity and confidentiality of all users' information is very important to us, and therefore we want to act responsibly in this area.

This Privacy Policy (hereinafter, the "Privacy Policy") describes the type of personal information we collect, how we obtain it, how we share it, and the purposes for which we process it, as well as the means available to you as a user to control such use and exercise your rights.

1. Scope of the Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the personal data obtained by Yatekomo from our customers, suppliers, information requesters, job applicants and, where applicable, users of our Website or third parties with whom we interact.

2. Who is the Data Controller?

The Data Controller for your data is GALLINA BLANCA, S.A. Below are our contact details: company name: GALLINA BLANCA, S.A.; contact email: dataprivacy@thegbfoods.com.

3. What information do we collect and how do we collect it?

We collect the information you give us directly, for example when you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us through the website, as well as basic technical data about your visit.

5. How long do we keep your data?

Purposes and retention periods
PurposeRetention period

For a reasonable period after your last interaction with us.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, the performance of any contract with you and our legitimate interest in answering your queries.

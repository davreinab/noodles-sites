Yatekomo Privacy Policy

At "Yatekomo" we place great importance on current regulations regarding privacy and data protection. In this sense, protecting the security, integrity and confidentiality of our users' information is very important to us, and therefore we want to act responsibly in this area.

This Privacy Policy (hereinafter, the "Privacy Policy") describes the type of personal information we collect, how we obtain it, how we share it, and the purposes for which we process it, as well as the means available to you as a user to control such use and exercise your rights.

1. Scope of the Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the personal data obtained by Yatekomo from our customers, suppliers, information requesters, job applicants and, where applicable, users of our Website or third parties with whom we may interact. This includes information collected offline, direct marketing campaigns, conferences and online events through our websites, as well as branded apps and pages on third-party platforms. If you have any questions regarding the processing and protection of your personal data, you can contact us at: dataprivacy@thegbfoods.com.

3. What information do we collect and how do we collect it?

In this Privacy Policy, your "personal information" refers to information or pieces of information that could allow you to be identified. As a general rule, it is not necessary to provide any personal information to visit our website. However, we collect personal information when: You share it with us through the contact forms on our Website or if you wish to take part in contests or other activities, in which case we request the personal information necessary to manage each specific activity. In these cases, we generally request identifying data such as name, address, username, profile picture, email address, contact phone number and, in the case of a legal entity, its corporate name and type of business. All personal information provided through the Website's forms must be truthful, authentic and up to date, with the user being solely responsible for any false or inaccurate information provided and for any damage this may cause to Yatekomo, where applicable. Fields marked with an asterisk are mandatory, so if they are not provided, we may deny the corresponding request. We also collect information that your web browser automatically sends us when you visit our website, in order to analyse your browsing behaviour and improve your experience; this includes, by way of example but not limited to, your IP address, consumption habits, and information about your preferences, such as your hobbies and interests. In addition, we also collect information gathered from other sources such as social media.

We inform you that we will process your data for the following purposes: To learn about your browsing preferences on our Website in order to personalise and improve our products and your experience. To achieve this purpose, we will carry out profiling based on your browsing habits on the website, on which no automated decisions will be made based on the profile generated. We inform you that, in relation to the creation and processing of data based on user profiling, through which we will personalise our products and improve your experience, you may exercise your right to object at any time, in accordance with the provisions of section 9 of this Privacy Policy. For more information, please see our Cookie Policy. To manage and process the response to your request for information sent through our Website's contact form. To handle emails received at our corporate email addresses for the purpose of maintaining business relationships with entities, organisations or companies and with the people who work for or collaborate with them or with their legal representatives. We will not use your data for any purpose other than those expressly set out in this Privacy Policy unless we have previously obtained the appropriate consent.

6. What legitimises us to process your data?

By using our Website, you accept that Yatekomo collects, uses and discloses your personal data in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the collection, use or disclosure of your personal data in this way, please refrain from using Yatekomo's Websites or provide your personal data to the relevant company in another way. In this regard, we process your personal data on the basis of the following legal bases: Consent given for this purpose by the data subject: for some processes, we will request your express, informed and freely given consent before processing, in order to ensure that you retain control over your data. Management of the contractual relationship. We will only collect, use or disclose your personal data when it is legitimate to do so. We will also request your explicit consent when we need to use your personal data for purposes not covered by this Privacy Policy. If you give us your consent, the terms of that consent will prevail in the event of any discrepancy with the terms of this Privacy Policy. Likewise, we will process your data in order to comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. How do we act with regard to the data of minors?

This Website is not directed at minors under 14 years of age, who must therefore refrain from providing any personal information. If we detect that we have accidentally collected personal data from minors under 14 years of age, we will proceed to delete it as soon as possible.

8. Who do we share your data with?

Your data will not be assigned, sold, rented or made available in any other way to any third party, except where (i) we have previously obtained the appropriate consent from users; (ii) it must be disclosed to official public bodies due to a legal obligation; or (iii) it may be necessary with respect to companies responsible for processing your personal data, such as IT hosting service providers or other providers that assist GBfoods. We may also share your personal information with other companies in the GBfoods group located in the European Economic Area, based on our corporate group's legitimate interest for internal administrative purposes. We take all reasonable precautions to keep your personal information secure and require third parties who handle or process your personal information to do the same.

9. What rights protect you regarding the processing of your data?

You have the right to obtain information about how we process your personal data. Specifically: You have the right to access your personal data to find out what personal data concerning you we are processing, as well as to request the rectification of inaccurate data or, where applicable, request its deletion when, among other reasons, the data is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected. In certain circumstances, you may request the restriction of the processing of your data, in which case we will only keep it for the exercise or defence of claims. In certain circumstances and for reasons related to your particular situation, you may object to the processing of your data. Yatekomo will stop processing your data, except for compelling legitimate reasons, or the exercise or defence of possible claims. You will also have the right to data portability as well as the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on the automated processing of your personal data. You can exercise these rights by sending an email to dataprivacy@thegbfoods.com with the reference "Data Protection", indicating the right you wish to exercise and enclosing a copy of your national ID card or similar identifying document proving your identity. We will respond to your requests as soon as possible and, in any case, within one (1) month of receiving your request. In certain circumstances, this period may be extended by a further two (2) months, in which case we will inform you accordingly within one (1) month of receiving your request. If you believe that the processing of your personal data infringes data protection regulations, or that we have not satisfied the exercise of your rights, you can file a complaint with the competent supervisory authority based on your place of residence.

10. Do you have the right to withdraw your consent?

Yes, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of the processing based on the consent prior to its withdrawal.

11. Is it possible to modify this Privacy Policy?

Our Privacy Policy may be subject to periodic modifications, with the updated version being published on our Website. We therefore invite you to check this page periodically. In this regard, we will inform you of any modification of particular relevance, and will request your consent for those processing activities subject to that legal basis.

12. Is there other Yatekomo privacy information?

In addition to this Privacy Policy, specific campaigns or promotions may be run, which will be governed by their own privacy terms or conditions set out in the legal terms of the contest or promotion in question. If you take part in them, we advise you to read those legal terms beforehand, as compliance with them will be required. Where applicable, we will make any additional terms and conditions available to you.
